# Privacy Policy for Finance Gatekeeper

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

## Who we are

Finance Gatekeeper is published by Abed In Tech. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at [negokamgaing@gmail.com](mailto:negokamgaing@gmail.com).

Finance Gatekeeper is a personal finance app intended for adults. It helps people record and review their finances. It is not a bank, lender, payment service, or provider of professional financial advice.

## Information the app handles

### Information stored on your device

The app stores the financial information you enter in its on-device database. This may include income and expense amounts, dates, categories, notes, account or wallet names and balances, budgets, debts and repayments, savings goals, planned purchases, recurring items, and related records.

The app also stores local settings, reminder preferences, and a salted hash of your optional four-digit app PIN. Your PIN itself is not stored. The PIN is separate from your optional cloud account.

The app can create on-device backup snapshots. These remain on your device unless you choose to export or share a file using your device's sharing features.

### Optional cloud account and backup

You can use the app without creating a cloud account. If you choose to create an account or sign in and use cloud backup, we handle your email address and the financial records you choose to sync. Synced records can include the categories and records described above. Cloud backup is used to back up and restore your records across devices.

Cloud account authentication and synced backups are provided through Supabase. The app stores the sign-in session using your device's secure storage. The app does not send your local PIN to Supabase.

### Information collected by the app automatically

The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics in the release covered by this policy. The app may use your device to schedule local reminders if you enable them. It does not use precise location to share your location with other users.

## How we use information

We use information to provide the app's finance tracking features, maintain your local preferences, authenticate optional cloud accounts, and sync or restore your backup when you request or enable those features.

## When information is shared

Your on-device financial records are not uploaded to cloud backup unless you choose to sign in and use the cloud sync feature. When you use cloud sync, relevant account and synced-record data is sent to Supabase to provide authentication, storage, backup, and restore.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your financial records for advertising. Service providers may process information on our behalf to provide the features you choose to use and under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

## Security

Cloud requests use encrypted network connections. Cloud data is associated with the signed-in account and access is restricted by the cloud service's account-level controls. The app stores the PIN as a salted hash on the device and stores the cloud sign-in session using the device's secure storage.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. The app's local database and snapshots are stored on your device; protect your device and use its screen-lock and security features.

## Retention and deletion

You can stop using cloud sync by signing out. Signing out does not delete your cloud account or its backup.

To request deletion of your cloud account and its associated synced records, open **Backup & restore** in the app and tap **Delete my account**, or email [negokamgaing@gmail.com](mailto:negokamgaing@gmail.com) from the email address used for the cloud account. Include **"Finance Gatekeeper account deletion"** in the subject. We will verify the request, then delete the cloud account and associated synced records. We will contact you if we need information to verify that you own the account.

Cloud deletion does not remotely erase a copy already stored on a device. You can remove local app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. On-device snapshots are also not removed by a cloud deletion request.

We delete the cloud account and its synced records within 30 days of verifying the request. Some limited information may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or records where required for security, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

See our [Account Deletion Instructions]({{ '/delete-account/' | relative_url }}) for the request steps.

## Children's privacy

Finance Gatekeeper is intended for adults and is not designed for children under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has created a cloud account, contact us at [negokamgaing@gmail.com](mailto:negokamgaing@gmail.com).
