# Finance Gatekeeper Account Deletion Instructions

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

Finance Gatekeeper cloud backup accounts are optional. You can use the app without signing in.

## Request deletion

To request deletion of your Finance Gatekeeper cloud account and the financial records associated with its cloud backup:

1. Email [negokamgaing@gmail.com](mailto:negokamgaing@gmail.com) from the email address registered to your Finance Gatekeeper cloud account.
2. Use **"Finance Gatekeeper account deletion"** as the subject.
3. We will verify account ownership. If we need more information to verify the request, we will contact you.
4. Within 30 days of verification, we will delete the cloud account and its associated synced records and confirm when the request has been processed.

In the app, **Backup & restore → Delete my account** opens this page.

Do not send your password or app PIN in the request.

## What deletion does and does not remove

This request covers the cloud account and records associated with its cloud backup. It does not remotely erase data already stored on a phone, including on-device backup snapshots. Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove its local data from your device.

Signing out only ends the current cloud session; it does not delete the cloud account or its backup. Some limited information may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or records where required for security, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

For questions, contact [negokamgaing@gmail.com](mailto:negokamgaing@gmail.com).
